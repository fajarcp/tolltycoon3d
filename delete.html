Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy. If you wish to have your personal data or account information deleted from Toll Tycoon 3D, please follow the instructions below.

1. Direct Deletion Request

To request the deletion of your data, please send an email to our support team:

✉️ Email: fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com

Please include your in-game username or Google Play account email so we can locate and remove your data. We will process your request within 7 business days.

2. Third-Party Ad Data (Unity Ads)

Our game uses Unity for analytics and advertising. To manage or delete your targeted advertising data collected by Unity:

🔗 Visit: Unity Privacy Portal or use the data privacy button located within the game's settings menu.
