Privacy Policy – Toll Tycoon 3D

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: FCP Developer Studio ("we", "us")
Contact: fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the mobile game Toll Tycoon 3D (the "Game") handles and how it is used. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do NOT collect

The Game has no accounts, no login and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (money, upgrades, settings such as sound on/off and your ad-privacy choice) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the Game or using "Reset progress" in Settings removes it.

3. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The Game is free and shows ads (rewarded video, interstitial and banner ads) provided by Unity Technologies through the Unity Ads SDK. To deliver, measure and secure ads, the SDK may collect and use:

DataWhy
Advertising ID and other device identifiersShow ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance, prevent fraud
IP address and approximate location derived from itShow ads relevant to your region, prevent fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, screen, network type, app version)Deliver ads that work on your device, analytics
Ad interactions (ad views, clicks, completions)Reward you for watching ads, measure and improve ads
Diagnostics (crash and performance data)Keep the ad service reliable

This data is processed by Unity as described in the Unity Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal data from Unity; we may see aggregated reports about ad performance.

Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: when you first open the Game you are asked whether personalised ads are allowed. You can change this at any time in Settings → Personalised ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less relevant and not based on your advertising ID.
  • Android settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you are never required to watch an ad to play.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the law of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help get it removed from the relevant service.

5. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone ourselves. The only third party that receives data through the Game is Unity Technologies (advertising), as described above.

6. Data retention and security

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Retention of advertising data is governed by Unity's policy. Data sent by the Unity SDK is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. International transfers

Unity may process data in countries other than your own. See the Unity Privacy Policy for details on safeguards.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests relate to Unity's ad data; you can email us at fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com and we will forward and help with your request, or you can contact Unity directly through its privacy portal.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
